# HeatShield AI — 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

This script allows judges or evaluators to experience the full value proposition of HeatShield AI in under 3 minutes.

---

## ⏱️ Step-by-Step Demo Flow

### 0:00 – 0:30 | Step 1: The Core Problem & First Impression
* **Action**: Open the application at `http://localhost:8000`.
* **Talking Point**:
  > *"Normal weather apps report that it is 42°C. But a 25-year-old software engineer in an air-conditioned office and a 67-year-old vendor or a construction laborer face completely different realities. HeatShield AI converts raw weather data into personal climate-survival intelligence."*
* **Visual**: Point to the **Hyperlocal Heat Risk Score (87/100 🔴 EXTREME)** on the hero dashboard.

---

### 0:30 – 0:55 | Step 2: Personal Vulnerability Differentiation
* **Action**: In the header **Demo Scenarios** dropdown, select **"Scenario 3: Outdoor Worker"**.
* **Talking Point**:
  > *"Watch how the exact same weather immediately updates the risk from 87 to 95/100. The Danger Window locks onto 12:30 PM – 4:30 PM. The system explains exactly why: continuous direct solar radiant load, unshaded asphalt radiation, and metabolic heat generation."*
* **Visual**: Highlight the updated recommendations (mandatory 30-min shaded rest cycles, 1 L water/hr quota).

---

### 0:55 – 1:25 | Step 3: Interactive Heat Map & Points of Interest
* **Action**: Click the **"Heat Map & POIs"** tab.
* **Talking Point**:
  > *"When heat is dangerous, where do you go? HeatShield AI maps nearby public drinking water stations (🚰), verified municipal cooling shelters (🌳), and emergency medical wards (🏥). Every location shows its exact distance, open hours, and transparent data source badge (Verified vs Demo)."*
* **Visual**: Click on the **RK Beach Refill Hub** and **Central Library AC Shelter** pins.

---

### 1:25 – 1:55 | Step 4: Heat-Safe Route Planner
* **Action**: Click the **"Heat-Safe Route"** tab and click **"Compare Fastest vs Heat-Safer Route"**.
* **Talking Point**:
  > *"Standard GPS navigators tell you the fastest route—usually across exposed, boiling asphalt. HeatShield AI calculates a Heat-Safer corridor that leverages tree canopy shade and public water points, delivering a 42% reduction in thermal radiant exposure for just 4 minutes of extra walking."*
* **Visual**: Show the side-by-side comparison cards and the Red vs Green path waypoints on the map.

---

### 1:55 – 2:25 | Step 5: Grounded AI Heat Assistant & Multilingual Voice
* **Action**: Click the **"AI Heat Assistant"** tab.
* **Action**: Click the prompt chip: *"I'm working outside tomorrow. When should I take breaks?"*
* **Talking Point**:
  > *"Our AI assistant never hallucinates weather or diagnoses diseases. It ingests the structured output of our deterministic risk engine. If the user mentions dizziness or vomiting, it immediately triggers critical emergency escalation."*
* **Action**: Switch language to **తెలుగు (Telugu)** or **हिन्दी (Hindi)** and enable the **TTS Voice Readout** to show multilingual accessibility.

---

### 2:25 – 2:55 | Step 6: Outdoor Worker Mode & City Command Center
* **Action**: Click the **"Outdoor Worker Mode"** tab.
  > *"Supervisors get an hourly shift safety matrix based on OSHA and NIOSH standards with exact water quotas."*
* **Action**: Click the **"City Command Center"** tab.
  > *"Municipal authorities and heat officers can view micro-zones ranked by Heat Vulnerability Index (HVI) with automated AI priority emergency dispatch recommendations (water tankers, work moratoriums, civic shelters)."*

---

### 2:55 – 3:00 | Step 7: Conclusion
* **Talking Point**:
  > *"HeatShield AI is not another weather app. It is a hyperlocal extreme heat risk, personal vulnerability, and action intelligence platform built to save lives. Predict. Protect. Prevent."*
